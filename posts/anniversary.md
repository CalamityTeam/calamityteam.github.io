<div class="text-center"><i>Well</i>, <b>10 years</b> is certainly a long time, isn’t it?</br>
 It's hard to believe that we have come so far from the days of the <a href="https://calamitymod.wiki.gg/wiki/1.0">Devourer being a purple Destroyer clone</a> and <a href="https://calamitymod.wiki.gg/wiki/1.1">Supreme Calamitas being an eyeball</a> that chased you halfway across the world.<br>Time sure flies when you’re developing a project as vast as Calamity.<br>Jokes aside, we thank you from the bottom of our hearts for continuously supporting us after all these years - though we may still have a long road ahead, we hope you will stick with us on our journey going forward.</div>

<h2 class="text-center">Announcing Version 2.3 - <a>Anniversary</a></h2>

<div class="text-center"><a>The Anniversary Update</a> is another update on the smaller side which will be something to behold. It will feature a significant from-the-ground-up boss rework and new custom UI styles!</div>
</br></br>
<div class="text-center">To celebrate its 10th birthday, our first boss, the <b>Desert Scourge will be receiving a complete rework!</b> Sporting a new, more reptilian design courtesy of Tobias and Triangle, our resident dune-dweller will now be found in the underground desert in its very own nest generation. The Scourge's aging drops are being reworked from the ground up to match this big makeover.</div>
</br></br>
<div class="text-center"><i>(You may see the very first iteration of Desert Scourge <a href="https://youtu.be/0NusDFPA63M">here</a>; this video was not produced by the Calamity Team and is included for historical purposes.)</i></div>
