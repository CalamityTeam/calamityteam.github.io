<h2 class="text-center">Announcing Version 2.3 - <a>Anniversary</a></h2>

<a>The Anniversary Update</a> is another update on the smaller side which will be something to behold. It will feature a significant from-the-ground-up boss rework and new custom UI styles!

To celebrate its 10th birthday, our very first boss, the Desert Scourge, will be receiving a complete rework! Sporting a new, more reptilian design courtesy of Tobias and Triangle, our resident dune-dweller will now be found in the underground desert in its very own nest generation. Alongside this, most of its older drops are also being reworked from the ground up to match this big makeover.
